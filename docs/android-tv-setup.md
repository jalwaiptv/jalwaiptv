# Android TV Setup Guide

Android TV and Google TV devices support a variety of media and IPTV-compatible applications through Google Play and other manufacturer-supported sources.

## Basic Setup

1. Connect the Android TV or Google TV device to the internet.
2. Install available system updates.
3. Open Google Play on the device.
4. Find a reputable compatible streaming application.
5. Install and launch the application.
6. Add the subscription information supplied for your account using the application's supported connection method.
7. Wait for the application to load its data and test playback.

## Recommended Checks

Confirm that the application supports your device version and the connection format supplied with your subscription. Keep both the operating system and application updated.

For improved stability, use Ethernet where practical or maintain a strong Wi-Fi signal.

## Troubleshooting

If playback buffers or stops frequently, restart the application and device, test your internet connection, and review the [Buffering Troubleshooting Guide](buffering-troubleshooting.md).
